Every movie and show you watch, in one place — seelogd is your watch diary, social network, and movie game in a single app.

🎬 TRACK
• Log the movies you watch and track TV shows episode by episode
• Rate, review, and take personal notes
• Automatic stats from your watch history: genres, hours, habits
• Discover your year with a personal "Wrapped" recap every December

📋 ORGANIZE
• Build a watchlist, favorites, and custom lists
• Create shared lists with friends and fill them together
• Get reminders for new episodes and release dates
• Home screen widgets put your lists and stats one glance away

👥 SHARE
• Add friends, browse their profiles and stats
• Share posts, comment, and like
• Plan movie nights in one-to-one and group chats
• Film Club: pick, watch and discuss a film together with your group

🎮 PLAY
• Test your movie knowledge: Scene Guess, Poster Puzzle, Blurred Poster and "Which came first?"
• Frame of the Day: name the film from a single frame — hints cost you points
• Film Chain: connect two actors through the films they share
• Question of the Day, daily duels, Cinema Bingo and Watch Challenges
• Vote in the Seelogd Top 100 and follow the monthly genre tournament
• Earn XP in the Weekly League, climb the leaderboards, collect badges

🤖 DISCOVER
• AI-powered recommendations tailored to your watch history
• "Where to watch?" — find which streaming platform carries what you're looking for

⭐ PREMIUM
The core features are free. Paid plans add:
• CineMatch AI: Free 3 per day / 30 per month, Premium 20 / 300, Premium Max 50 / 900 messages
• A plan-specific profile identity badge your friends can see
• Higher limits: custom lists (Free 1 · Premium 25 · Max unlimited), friends (2 · 150 · unlimited), group chats (1 · 10 · unlimited), notes and to-do lists (5 · 50 · unlimited), note length (500 · 2,000 · 20,000 characters), movie and show notes (20 · 200 · unlimited), movie and show reminders (10 and 20 · 50 and 100 · unlimited)
• Day-and-time reminders for your notes and to-do lists
• Games (Scene Guess, Poster Puzzle and the quick games), Film Chain, Frame of the Day, Film Club, CineSwipe and voting in the monthly tournament — Free can watch the tournament, while Question of the Day and the Weekly League are open to everyone
• Your own themes, all 56 fonts, app icon picker, snowfall, ambient background and icon background
• Wrapped archive (Free this year only · Premium last 5 years · Max every year) and streaming alerts for up to 100 titles (Free: 10)
• In-app bug reports (Premium and Max) and feature requests (Max only)

Available in English, Turkish, Spanish, Portuguese and German. Dark theme included. Free to use.
