Every movie and show you watch, in one place. seelogd is your watch diary, your stats, your friends and your movie games in a single app.

🎬 TRACK
• Log movies, follow TV shows episode by episode and never lose your place
• Rate out of 10 with half points (like 8.5), write reviews and private notes
• Weekly streak: watch on two days a week and your flame turns a new color every week
• Reminders for new episodes and release dates, with one-tap add to your phone calendar
• Home screen widgets keep your lists and stats one glance away

📊 STATS
• Total watch time, genre breakdown, rating distribution and daily history
• Episode rating graphs for TV shows
• 160+ badges and a personal Wrapped recap at the end of the year

📋 ORGANIZE
• Watchlist, favorites, your four favorites and custom lists
• Shared lists you build together with friends
• Import from Letterboxd and IMDb; export your library as JSON or CSV

🔎 DISCOVER
• Trending, top rated, upcoming, World Tour, Oscar and Emmy winners
• "What should I watch?" filters and CineMatch AI picks based on your history
• Where to watch: see which platform carries a title and get notified when it arrives

👥 SHARE
• Add friends, browse their profiles and lists, see your taste match
• Share posts, comment, and plan movie nights in one-to-one and group chats
• Film Club: pick, watch and discuss a film together with your group

🎮 PLAY
• Scene Guess, Poster Puzzle, Blurred Poster, "Which came first?" and "Which is rated higher?"
• Frame of the Day, Film Chain, Cinema Bingo and Watch Challenges
• Question of the Day, the Weekly League, a monthly genre tournament and the seelogd Top 100

🎨 MAKE IT YOURS
• Light and dark themes, build your own theme, dozens of fonts
• Atmosphere: living backgrounds inspired by the seasons and the stories you love
• App icon picker

⭐ PREMIUM
Tracking, lists, stats, discovery and chat are free. Paid plans (Premium and Premium Max) add:
• More CineMatch AI messages (Free 3 a day, Premium 20, Max 50)
• Games, Film Chain, Frame of the Day, Film Club, CineSwipe and voting in the tournament
• Higher limits for lists, friends, groups, notes and reminders
• Theme studio, every font, the icon picker and ambient backgrounds
• Wrapped archive for past years and a plan badge on your profile

Available in English, Turkish, Spanish, Portuguese, German, Italian, French and Japanese.

seelogd does not host or stream any movies or shows.
