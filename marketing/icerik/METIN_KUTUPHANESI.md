## Reddit ana gönderi

Başlık:

```text
I'm building a movie & TV tracker and looking for beta testers (Android & iOS)
```

Metin:

```text
Hi everyone,

I'm the solo developer of **seelogd**, a movie and TV app for Android and iOS. It's almost ready, and before launch I'd like people who actually track what they watch to try it and tell me what's wrong with it.

**What it does**

- Track movies and shows episode by episode, with ratings, reviews and private notes
- Import your history from Letterboxd or IMDb, and export your library any time
- Filter by runtime, genre, era and streaming platform, and get an alert when a title reaches your service
- Add friends, build shared lists and plan movie night in group chats with polls
- Play movie games, like guessing a film from a single frame
- Get a shareable recap of your year
- Use it in English, Turkish, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese or German

**What it costs**

It's free to start, and your watched list, watchlist and favourites are unlimited. Games, more friends and more custom lists are part of optional paid plans. I'd rather say that upfront.

It doesn't host or stream anything. Movie and TV data comes from TMDB, and streaming availability comes from JustWatch.

**How to join**

1. Go to [seelogd.com/#beta](https://seelogd.com/#beta)
2. Choose Android or iOS and leave your email
3. I'll email you an invite as spots open

On Android, use the email of the Google account signed in to Play Store. On iOS, use your Apple ID email, as the invite comes through TestFlight. Test builds may contain bugs.

Don't want to test? You can join the waitlist at [seelogd.com](https://seelogd.com) instead. You'll get one email on launch day, nothing else.

**What I'd love to hear**

What's confusing, what breaks, and what your current app does better. Harsh feedback is welcome, and I'll be in the comments to answer questions.
```
